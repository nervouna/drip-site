Frequently asked questions

How do I sync between devices?

Turn on iCloud Sync in Settings › Privacy & Sync on each iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account. If Settings asks you to sign in, sign in to iCloud in the Settings app.

How do I get my data out?

Go to Settings › Privacy & Sync › Export Data. All your entries are exported as a CSV file that you can share from the iOS share sheet.

Can you see my data?

No. Your data stays on your iPhone (and in your own iCloud when sync is on). The developer runs no servers and has no access to it.

How do I add widgets?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, then search for Drip.
